Our Mission

Conserving the lands,
oceans, and stories
on which all life depends

Earth Ark Institute is a non-profit research and experience organization dedicated to making ecological science accessible and emotionally resonant. We design immersive environments where visitors don't just learn about nature — they step into it, feel it, and carry it home.

Our approach combines rigorous scientific research with cutting-edge spatial design and interactive technology, creating experiences that transform knowledge into lasting conservation action.

🌊

Science-Driven

Every project is grounded in peer-reviewed research and developed with academic partners.

🦖

Immersive by Design

We build physical and digital spaces that transform data into embodied experience.

💡

Open & Collaborative

We partner with communities, schools, and governments to maximize impact.

About Us

Who We Are

Earth Ark Institute is a dedicated team of scientists, architects, technologists, and storytellers working together to advance lasting conservation across 80+ countries and territories.

We develop breakthrough ideas, amplify local leaders, and influence policy to address the climate and biodiversity crises between now and 2030. Our projects are not one-off exhibitions but foundational experiences in a decade-long initiative to reimagine humanity's relationship with the natural world.
