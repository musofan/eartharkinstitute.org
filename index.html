Earth Ark Institute

We design and build immersive experiences that bridge the gap between human understanding and the natural world. Through the intersection of ecology, architecture, and technology, we create spaces that inspire stewardship of our planet.

Selected Work

Immersive Projects

CASE 01

T.Rex Immersive Exhibition

Beijing · 1000㎡ · 2024

An educational journey through the Late Cretaceous period, combining paleontological accuracy with interactive RFID exploration systems for young learners.

CASE 02

Deep Sea Ark Program

Shanghai · Multi-site · 2023

Underwater ecosystem conservation initiative featuring large-scale immersive installations that simulate deep-sea environments and marine biodiversity.

CASE 03

DreamPort Aerospace

Dongguan · 2000㎡ · 2024

Self-developed hardware and immersive flight simulation system exploring humanity's relationship with space exploration and aerospace technology.

CASE 04

Zhongshan Park Immersive Complex

Shanghai · 5400㎡ · 2025

Shanghai 2035 flagship project — a co-investment model with the Changning District government creating a next-generation public cultural space.

Our Values

Why We Exist

01

Stewardship Through Immersion

We believe that direct, embodied experience of natural wonders fosters deeper commitment to conservation than information alone.

02

Scientific Integrity

Every exhibition is grounded in rigorous research, developed in collaboration with academic institutions including Columbia, MIT Media Lab, and Carnegie Mellon.

03

Technology as Bridge

We leverage immersive technologies not for spectacle, but as tools to make the invisible visible — from microscopic ecosystems to geological time scales.

04

Ten-Year Commitment

Earth Ark Institute is not a one-off concept but a decade-long IP initiative by NeXT SCENE. This exhibition is the first ark — many more will follow.
